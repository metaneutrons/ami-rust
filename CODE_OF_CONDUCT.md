# Community conduct

Participation in this project requires respectful, professional discussion.
Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, and deliberate exposure
of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove material that violates this policy and may limit
participation where necessary to keep the project usable. Report a concern to a
repository maintainer through the hosting platform's available private contact
route. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue.
