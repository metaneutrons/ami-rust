---
title: Qualification
description: What 0.1.0 was demonstrated to do.
---

The 0.1.0 release was qualified on all three supported development hosts and both AROS x86-64 ABI targets. The exact release candidate produced target ELF binaries, completed dual-target Cargo builds, and passed native AROS execution checks.

This is a specific claim, not a broad promise of arbitrary Rust-on-AROS compatibility. It does not extend to full `std`, a native AROS compiler host, unqualified CPUs, or libraries outside the documented crate surface.

Detailed acceptance records are versioned with the source in the repository's [qualification documents](https://github.com/metaneutrons/ami-rust/tree/main/docs).
